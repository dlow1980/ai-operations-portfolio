# Customer Support FAQ & Knowledge Base

## 1. Account & Profile Management

### How do I update my billing card or payment method?
Navigate to **Settings > Billing & Invoices** from your workspace dashboard[cite: 3]. Under **Payment Methods**, select **Edit Payment Method** or **Add New Card**[cite: 3]. Enter your updated card details and click **Save Changes**[cite: 3]. Changes take effect immediately[cite: 3].

### How do I change my account login email?
Go to **Settings > Account Details**, enter your new email under **Email Address**, and click **Save**[cite: 3]. A verification link will be sent to confirm the update[cite: 3].

### How do I reset my password?
On the sign-in page, click **Forgot Password** and enter your registered email address[cite: 3].

---

## 2. Subscription & Invoicing

### Where can I download past invoices?
In your dashboard, open **Settings > Billing & Invoices**[cite: 3]. Scroll to **Invoice History** to download PDF receipts[cite: 3].

### Can I switch between monthly and annual plans?
Under **Settings > Subscription**, click **Change Plan**[cite: 3]. Annual billing includes a 20% discount[cite: 3].

### What is the refund policy?
We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for new subscriptions[cite: 3]. Requests beyond 14 days or contract cancellations require escalation to a Team Lead[cite: 3].

## 3. Troubleshooting & Technical

### Where can I generate API keys?
Go to **Developer Settings > API Keys** and select **Create New Secret Key**[cite: 3].

### Why am I getting a 429 Too Many Requests error?
The rate limit is 60 requests per minute on standard plans[cite: 3]. Implement exponential backoff or upgrade for higher limits[cite: 3].
